# Academic editing / 学术表达

Apply alongside the prose's language reference. Keep the discipline's register and the author's
argument. Academic does not mean uniformly dense, passive, informal or conversational.

| Preserve | Check during revision |
|---|---|
| Population, conditions, exclusions and comparison groups | Which group a modifier restricts; necessary versus sufficient conditions; exceptions |
| Evidence strength and attribution | Association versus causation, possibility versus certainty, observed absence versus proof of absence |
| Numbers, units, ranges and statistical notation | Inequalities, bounds, denominators, confidence intervals and significance must not drift |
| Terms, abbreviations and operational definitions | Keep one concept stable; keep the first definition and distinct concepts distinct |
| Citations, formulas and cross-references | Keep a citation attached to its supported claim, not merely present somewhere in the paragraph |
| Section function | Methods explain procedure; results state findings; discussion may qualify them; an abstract can repeat needed information |

Some hedges encode different limits. Remove repeated wording only after identifying what each
limit does. A missing source is not permission to invent one, erase the limitation, or silently
delete a substantive claim. Flag a consequential uncertainty separately. Do not convert an
editing request into a new literature review or a rewrite of the research findings.

中文：“仅在低照度条件下可能降低误差，室外尚未验证。”不能改成“能降低误差”。
“未发现差异的证据”不等于“证明两组相同”；“超过 30%”不等于“30%”。

English: “No evidence of a difference was found in this sample” is not “The groups are
equivalent”. “Participants were excluded only if X” is not “All participants with X were
excluded”. Preserve unknown actors in passives when the source does not identify them.

Nominalizations and long noun phrases can be efficient for specialist readers. Expand them
when they hide a necessary relationship, not merely to make the text less academic. Keep
the original's spelling convention or the stated journal style. Do not change equations,
citation keys, heading anchors or table data while polishing surrounding language.
